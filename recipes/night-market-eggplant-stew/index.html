Night Market Eggplant Stew

Eggplant, potato, and red pepper in a red curry coconut broth with lime and basil.

Prep 15 min · Cook 20 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 1 shallot, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 2 tablespoons vegan red curry paste
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 large potato, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 large eggplant, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1/2 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1/4 cup Thai basil leaves

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and heat the coconut oil. Cook the shallot, garlic, and ginger for 2 minutes until fragrant, then stir in the curry paste and cook it for 1 minute.
  2. Pour in 1 cup water and scrape up anything stuck to the bottom of the pot. Press Cancel.
  3. Add the potato, eggplant, and bell pepper and stir to coat them in the curry base.
  4. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook the eggplant and potato on High for 4 minutes. Quick release the pressure; the potato should be fork-tender and the eggplant silky.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk and soy sauce, then the lime juice and basil, and serve the stew hot.
