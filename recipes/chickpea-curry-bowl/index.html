Monsoon Curry Bowl

Potatoes, cauliflower, and chickpeas in a Jamaican curry and coconut sauce, ready in one pot.

Prep 15 min · Cook 20 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 scallions, sliced, greens reserved
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon Jamaican curry powder
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 cups diced potatoes (1-inch)
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 1 cup light coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and heat the coconut oil. Cook the onion until soft, about 4 minutes, then stir in the garlic, scallion whites, thyme, and curry powder and cook them for 1 minute.
  2. Pour in 1 cup water and scrape the bottom of the pot clean. Press Cancel.
  3. Add the potatoes, then the chickpeas and salt, and set the cauliflower florets on top.
  4. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook the potatoes and cauliflower on High for 4 minutes. Quick release the pressure.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk and lime juice; the potatoes should be fork-tender and the cauliflower soft.
  6. Spoon the curry into bowls and top with the scallion greens.
