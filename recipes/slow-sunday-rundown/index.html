Slow Sunday Rundown

A Jamaican rundown: potatoes, cauliflower, and chickpeas simmered until the coconut sauce runs down thick.

Prep 15 min · Cook 30 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 2 cups diced potatoes (1-inch)
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 can (13.5 ounces) light coconut milk
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 teaspoon low-sodium tamari
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and heat the coconut oil. Cook the onion until soft, about 4 minutes, then stir in the garlic, scallions, thyme, and allspice for 1 minute.
  2. Add the potatoes, 1 cup water, and salt and scrape the bottom of the pot. Press Cancel.
  3. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook the potatoes on High for 3 minutes. Quick release the pressure.
  4. Select Sauté (medium) again and stir in the coconut milk, cauliflower, chickpeas, and tamari. Simmer the rundown uncovered, stirring often, for 10-12 minutes until the potatoes and cauliflower are tender and the coconut sauce thickens.
  5. Press Cancel, stir in the lime juice, and serve the rundown hot.
