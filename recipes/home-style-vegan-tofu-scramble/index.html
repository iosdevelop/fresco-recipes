Bright Morning Tofu Scramble

Turmeric tofu scramble with crisp golden potatoes, scallions, and a squeeze of lime.

Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups diced potatoes (1/2-inch)
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 14 ounces extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup light coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro

Instructions

  1. Add the potatoes and 1/2 cup water to the Instant Pot. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook the potatoes on High for 2 minutes. Quick release the pressure; the potatoes should be just tender.
  2. Pour off any remaining water, select Sauté (medium-high), and add the coconut oil. Brown the potatoes, stirring only every few minutes, until golden and crisp at the edges, 6-8 minutes.
  3. Push the potatoes to the side, add the scallions and garlic, and cook them for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Crumble in the tofu, sprinkle with the turmeric, thyme, allspice, pepper, and salt, and stir everything together. Cook the scramble for 4-5 minutes, then stir in the coconut milk until creamy.
  5. Press Cancel, squeeze over the lime juice, and scatter with cilantro before serving.
