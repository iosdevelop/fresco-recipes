Island Breeze Coconut Rice

Toasted brown rice cooked in coconut milk with garlic, thyme, and scallion, brightened with lime.

Prep 10 min · Cook 40 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown rice
  • 1 teaspoon coconut oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 scallions, sliced, whites and greens separated
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Rinse the brown rice in a fine-mesh strainer until the water runs clear, then drain it well.
  2. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot, add the coconut oil, and toast the drained rice with the garlic and scallion whites for 2 minutes until fragrant. Press Cancel.
  3. Add the coconut milk, 1 cup water, thyme, and salt and stir, scraping the bottom so no rice sticks.
  4. Lock the lid and pressure cook the rice on High for 22 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then press the Venting button for Quick Release and open the lid.
  5. Fluff the rice with a fork, fold in the lime juice and scallion greens, and serve.
