Ember Jerk Potato Bowl

Jerk-marinated potatoes and peppers, air-fried until charred, over brown rice with a coconut-lime drizzle.

Prep 20 min · Cook 45 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown rice, rinsed
  • 2 cups water
  • 3 cups diced potatoes (1-inch)
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 1/2 Scotch bonnet pepper, seeded and minced (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 tablespoon low-sodium tamari
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil, melted
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup light coconut milk
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice, divided

Instructions

  1. Add the brown rice and 2 cups water to the rice cooker and cook the rice on the Brown Rice setting, about 45 minutes.
  2. Whisk the scallions, garlic, ginger, Scotch bonnet, thyme, allspice, tamari, coconut oil, salt, and 1 tablespoon lime juice into a jerk paste. Toss the potatoes and bell pepper in the paste and let them marinate for 10 minutes.
  3. Air fry the potatoes and bell pepper at 390°F for 18-20 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until the potatoes are fork-tender inside and charred at the edges.
  4. Whisk the coconut milk with the remaining lime juice for a quick drizzle.
  5. Fluff the rice, divide it among bowls, top it with the jerk potatoes and peppers, and spoon over the coconut-lime drizzle.
