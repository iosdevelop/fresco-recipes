Spice Market Samosas

Crisp air-fried samosas filled with spiced potatoes and peas, finished with lime and cilantro.

Prep 30 min · Cook 35 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil, plus more for brushing
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro
  • 12 vegan samosa wrappers

Instructions

  1. Add the potatoes and 1 cup water to the Instant Pot. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook the potatoes on High for 3 minutes. Quick release the pressure; the potatoes should be fork-tender.
  2. Drain the potatoes and set them aside. Dry the inner pot, select Sauté (medium), and heat the olive oil. Toast the cumin seeds for 30 seconds, then cook the onion until golden, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, curry powder, and garam masala and cook them for 1 minute, then add the tomato and cook it until soft, about 2 minutes.
  4. Add the potatoes, peas, and salt, lightly mash a few potato pieces, and cook the filling for 2 minutes until the peas are hot. Press Cancel and stir in the lime juice and cilantro.
  5. Let the filling cool for 10 minutes. Spoon 2 tablespoons onto each wrapper, fold it into a triangle, and seal the edges with a dab of water.
  6. Brush the samosas with olive oil and air fry the samosas in batches at 375°F for 8-10 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp.
