Firecracker Jerk Tofu Bowl

Air-fried jerk tofu and charred cauliflower over brown rice with creamy coconut black beans.

Prep 25 min · Cook 45 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown rice, rinsed
  • 1 1/4 cups water
  • 14 ounces extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 1/2 Scotch bonnet pepper, seeded and minced (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 tablespoon low-sodium tamari
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice, divided
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil, melted
  • 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup light coconut milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Add the brown rice and 1 1/4 cups water to the Instant Pot. Lock the lid and pressure cook the rice on High for 22 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes and press the Venting button for Quick Release. Fluff the rice, spoon it into a covered bowl, and rinse the inner pot.
  2. Whisk the scallions, garlic, ginger, Scotch bonnet, thyme, allspice, tamari, 1 tablespoon lime juice, and coconut oil into a jerk marinade. Toss the tofu and cauliflower in the marinade and let them marinate for 15 minutes.
  3. Air fry the tofu and cauliflower in a single layer at 390°F for 14 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until the tofu is crisp and the cauliflower is fork-tender and charred at the edges.
  4. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and simmer the black beans with the coconut milk and salt for 5 minutes until creamy. Press Cancel.
  5. Fluff the rice, divide it among bowls, and top it with the black beans, jerk tofu, and cauliflower. Finish with the remaining lime juice.
