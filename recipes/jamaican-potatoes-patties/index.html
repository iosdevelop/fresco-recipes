Crackle Potato Patties

Crisp oven-baked patties of mashed potato, cauliflower, and chickpeas seasoned with thyme and allspice.

Prep 20 min · Cook 35 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups diced potatoes (1-inch)
  • 1 cup cauliflower florets
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil, plus more for brushing
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 scallions, finely sliced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 teaspoon low-sodium tamari
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1/2 cup oat flour

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Pour 1 cup water into the Instant Pot, set in the steamer rack, and add the potatoes and cauliflower.
  3. Lock the lid and steam the potatoes and cauliflower on High for 5 minutes. Press the Venting button for Quick Release; the potatoes should be fork-tender.
  4. Lift out the vegetables, pour out the water, and select Sauté (medium). Heat the coconut oil and cook the onion until soft, about 4 minutes, then stir in the garlic, scallions, thyme, and allspice for 1 minute. Press Cancel.
  5. In a large bowl, mash the potatoes, cauliflower, and chickpeas until mostly smooth. Mix in the onion mixture, tamari, salt, lime juice, and oat flour until the mixture holds together.
  6. Shape the mixture into 8 patties about 1/2 inch thick, set them on the sheet pan, and brush the tops with a little coconut oil.
  7. Bake the patties for 20 minutes, flipping them halfway, until golden and crisp at the edges.
