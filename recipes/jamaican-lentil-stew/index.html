Firelight Lentil Stew

Lentils, carrots, and potatoes in a thyme and allspice broth enriched with coconut milk.

Prep 15 min · Cook 35 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 cup dried brown lentils, rinsed
  • 2 cups diced potatoes (1-inch)
  • 3 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup light coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and heat the olive oil. Cook the onion and carrots until the onion softens, about 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the garlic, thyme, and allspice and cook them for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the lentils, potatoes, 3 cups water, and salt, scraping the bottom of the pot. Press Cancel.
  4. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook the lentils and potatoes on High for 10 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes.
  5. Stir the coconut milk and lime juice into the stew; the lentils should be soft and the potatoes fork-tender. Serve hot.
