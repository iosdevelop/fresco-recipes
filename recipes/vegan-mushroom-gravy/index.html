Velvet Mushroom Gravy

A silky, savory gravy of browned mushrooms, thyme, and tamari for rice, mash, or dumplings.

Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 8 ounces cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 tablespoon tamari
  • 1 teaspoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium-high) on the Instant Pot and heat the olive oil. Cook the mushrooms without stirring for 3 minutes, then stir and cook the mushrooms until browned, about 4 minutes more.
  2. Add the onion and cook it until soft, about 3 minutes, then stir in the garlic, thyme, and pepper for 30 seconds.
  3. Sprinkle the flour over the mushrooms and stir constantly for 1 minute so it loses its raw taste.
  4. Slowly whisk in the vegetable stock and tamari, scraping up the browned bits. Simmer the gravy, stirring often, until it coats a spoon, 4-5 minutes.
  5. Press Cancel, stir in the lime juice, and serve the gravy warm.
