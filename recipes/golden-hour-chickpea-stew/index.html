Golden Hour Chickpea Stew

Sweet potatoes and chickpeas simmered with thyme, allspice, and light coconut milk, finished with lime.

Prep 15 min · Cook 25 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 scallions, sliced, whites and greens separated
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup light coconut milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and heat the olive oil. Cook the onion and scallion whites until soft, about 4 minutes.
  2. Stir in the garlic, thyme, allspice, and curry powder and cook them for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in 1 cup water and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Press Cancel.
  4. Add the sweet potatoes, chickpeas, coconut milk, and salt and stir to combine.
  5. Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook the sweet potatoes and chickpeas on High for 5 minutes. Quick release the pressure.
  6. Check the sweet potatoes with a fork: they should be fork-tender. Mash a few pieces against the side of the pot to thicken the stew.
  7. Stir in the lime juice and scallion greens and serve the stew hot.
