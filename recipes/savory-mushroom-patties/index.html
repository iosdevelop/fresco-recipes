Bonfire Mushroom Patties

Crispy air-fried patties of browned mushrooms and tofu with thyme, allspice, and lime.

Prep 20 min · Cook 25 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces cremini mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil, melted, divided
  • 14 ounces extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 scallions, finely sliced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 teaspoon low-sodium tamari
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1/2 cup oat flour

Instructions

  1. Toss the mushrooms and onion with half the coconut oil, spread them in the air fryer basket, and air fry the mushrooms and onion at 375°F for 8 minutes, stirring once, until browned and dry.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the tofu with a fork, then mix in the mushrooms and onion, garlic, scallions, thyme, allspice, tamari, salt, lime juice, and oat flour until the mixture holds together.
  3. Shape the mixture into 8 patties about 1/2 inch thick and brush both sides with the remaining coconut oil.
  4. Air fry the patties at 375°F for 14 minutes, flipping them after 7 minutes, until browned and crisp at the edges.
  5. Rest the patties for 3 minutes before serving.
