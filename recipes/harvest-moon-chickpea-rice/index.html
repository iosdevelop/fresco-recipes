Harvest Moon Chickpea Rice

Brown rice and chickpeas cooked with tomatoes, smoked paprika, and cumin, finished with lemon and parsley.

Prep 10 min · Cook 50 min · Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 sweet onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 2 cups brown rice, rinsed
  • 2 1/2 cups water
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup mixed frozen vegetables
  • 1/2 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and heat the olive oil. Cook the onion until soft, about 4 minutes, then stir in the garlic, paprika, and cumin for 1 minute.
  2. Add the brown rice and stir to coat it in the spiced oil for 1 minute. Press Cancel.
  3. Pour in 2 1/2 cups water and the diced tomatoes, scrape the bottom of the pot clean, and scatter the chickpeas and salt on top without stirring.
  4. Lock the lid and pressure cook the rice and chickpeas on High for 22 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then press the Venting button for Quick Release and open the lid.
  5. Stir in the frozen vegetables and coconut milk, close the lid for 5 minutes so the vegetables warm through, then fold in the lemon juice and parsley.
