Sunday Rice and Peas

Classic Jamaican rice and peas: brown rice and kidney beans cooked in coconut milk with thyme, scallion, and allspice.

Prep 10 min · Cook 45 min · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown rice, rinsed
  • 1 can red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Select Sauté (medium) on the Instant Pot and toast the garlic, scallions, thyme, and allspice in 2 tablespoons of the coconut milk for 1 minute until fragrant. Press Cancel.
  2. Add the brown rice, kidney beans, remaining coconut milk, 1 cup water, and salt. Stir once so the rice sits under the liquid.
  3. Lock the lid and pressure cook the rice and peas on High for 22 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then press the Venting button for Quick Release and open the lid.
  4. Fluff the rice and peas with a fork, stir in the lime juice, and serve.
